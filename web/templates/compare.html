Compare

Companies as columns, one metric catalog, deterministic financial facts only — no LLM inference.

Pick up to 4 companies to see them side by side across the same ratios shown on each company's own Overview tab, one period (the latest) per company. Mixing an Indian and a US company automatically converts monetary figures to USD, using a live exchange rate noted below the table — ratios and percentages (P/E, ROE, margins, growth, ...) need no conversion and are shown as-is either way.

Pick any number of attributes to see the full recorded history side by side for the companies selected in Quick Comparison — not a spec-sheet snapshot, the same per-period series the Charts tab plots, laid out as a table instead.

The same assumptions-driven Growth Projection model as each company's own Valuation Model tab — adjust required return, growth, and price assumptions and see the impact on intrinsic value and margin of safety, for whichever company below you've selected in Quick Comparison.
